A few good questions

Frequently asked.

Can I try Nexdo before I pay? +

Yes. Free stays free for as long as you want it, and Pro and Max both open with a 14-day trial that needs no credit card.

What's the difference between Pro and Max? +

Pro gives you the full planning assistant — briefings, next-step recommendations, voice, Fix My Day. Max adds personalization that learns from your own pace: duration estimates, work-pattern insights and deeper analytics.

Can I change plans later? +

Any time, in both directions. Upgrades take effect immediately and downgrades at the end of your billing period.

Is my data secure? +

Your tasks and calendar are yours. Personalization is opt-in, AI changes wait for your approval, and you can export or delete your data whenever you like.

Do you offer refunds? +

The 14-day trial is there so you don't need one. If something goes wrong after you're billed, contact support and we'll sort it out.

Do you offer team or business plans? +

Team plans are in the works. Get in touch and we'll tell you when they're ready.
