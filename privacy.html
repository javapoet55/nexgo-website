Nexdo respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how Nexdo (“Nexdo,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Nexdo mobile application, website, AI assistant, and related products and services (collectively, the “Services”).

Nexdo is designed to help you organize tasks, calendars, reminders, communications, and daily activities and, when you choose, help you take actions related to them.

Your personal data belongs to you. Nexdo should access only the information necessary to provide the features you choose to use.

You stay in control. You can choose which integrations and permissions to enable and may revoke those permissions at any time.

01Information We Collect

The information Nexdo collects depends on which features you choose to use.

1.1Account Information

When you create a Nexdo account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Profile information
  • Authentication information
  • Subscription status
  • Preferences and settings

If you sign in through Apple, Google, or another identity provider, we receive the information that provider makes available according to the permissions you grant.

1.2Tasks and Productivity Information

When you create or manage tasks, Nexdo may process information including:

  • Task titles and descriptions
  • Due dates
  • Priority
  • Reminders
  • Notes
  • Projects
  • Categories
  • Completion status
  • Recurring-task settings
  • Scheduling preferences

This information is used to provide task management, reminders, scheduling, prioritization, AI recommendations, and related features.

1.3Calendar Information

If you connect a supported calendar or grant Nexdo calendar permissions, Nexdo may access information such as:

  • Event titles
  • Date and time
  • Duration
  • Availability
  • Location
  • Attendees
  • Event notes
  • Calendar identifiers

We use calendar information to provide features such as:

  • Daily and weekly planning
  • Conflict detection
  • Available-time recommendations
  • Task scheduling
  • Appointment creation
  • Reminders
  • Daily briefings
  • AI scheduling recommendations

Nexdo does not require access to your calendar unless you choose to enable features that need it. Whenever practical, Nexdo may process scheduling information locally on your device or minimize the information transmitted to external systems.

1.4Contacts

If you give Nexdo permission to access contacts, we may process information such as:

  • Contact name
  • Phone number
  • Email address

For example, if you create the task “Call Damien at 10 AM,” Nexdo may use your contacts to determine which Damien you intended.

We do not use your address book to build advertising profiles or market to your contacts.

1.5Email Information

If you connect an email account, Nexdo may process email information necessary to provide the features you request.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • Sender
  • Recipient
  • Subject
  • Message content
  • Date and time
  • Thread information
  • Attachments
  • Message identifiers

For example, Nexdo may allow you to:

  • Summarize recent emails
  • Search emails
  • Draft responses
  • Create tasks from email
  • Send an email you approve

Nexdo only accesses connected email accounts according to the permissions you grant.

1.6Voice and Audio

If you use Nexdo’s voice features, we may process:

  • Audio you provide
  • Speech transcripts
  • Voice commands
  • AI conversation context

Voice information may be transmitted to service providers that assist Nexdo with speech recognition, speech generation, or AI processing. Unless we specifically disclose otherwise and obtain any consent required by law, Nexdo does not use your private voice recordings to identify you biometrically.

1.7AI Assistant Content

When you interact with Nexdo’s AI features, we may process:

  • Your prompts
  • Relevant task information
  • Relevant calendar context
  • Relevant email or contact information when necessary
  • AI responses
  • Feedback you provide about AI responses

Nexdo seeks to send only the information reasonably necessary to fulfill your request.

1.8Actions You Ask Nexdo to Perform

Nexdo may allow you to request actions such as:

  • Creating a task
  • Creating or changing a calendar event
  • Calling a contact
  • Preparing or sending an email
  • Preparing or sending a message
  • Scheduling an appointment
  • Contacting a business
  • Creating reminders
  • Rescheduling tasks

Where appropriate, Nexdo will request your confirmation before taking an external action. For example, for a task such as “Call Damien at 10:00 AM,” Nexdo may ask whether you want to initiate the call before doing so. Permissions and confirmation requirements may vary depending on your device, operating system, and integration provider.

1.9Device and Usage Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Language
  • Time zone
  • Crash reports
  • Performance information
  • Feature usage
  • Interaction events
  • Diagnostic logs

We use this information primarily to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Nexdo.

1.10Payment Information

If you purchase a Nexdo subscription, payments may be processed by:

  • Apple
  • Google
  • Stripe
  • Another authorized payment provider

Nexdo generally does not receive your full payment-card number. We may receive information such as your subscription tier, transaction identifier, billing status, renewal status, and purchase history.

02How Nexdo Uses Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Provide Nexdo features
  • Synchronize tasks and calendars
  • Generate reminders
  • Detect schedule conflicts
  • Recommend available time
  • Prioritize tasks
  • Provide daily and weekly briefings
  • Generate AI responses
  • Process voice commands
  • Perform actions you request
  • Integrate with third-party services
  • Provide customer support
  • Detect fraud or abuse
  • Maintain security
  • Diagnose problems
  • Improve product reliability
  • Develop new features
  • Process subscriptions
  • Comply with legal requirements

03Our AI Privacy Principles

3.1AI Uses Only Relevant Context

When possible, Nexdo limits AI processing to information relevant to the request. For example, if you ask “When am I free tomorrow afternoon?”, the AI does not need your entire historical calendar.

3.2Your Private Content Is Not Public Data

Your tasks, calendar events, email content, contacts, and private AI conversations remain your information.

3.3AI Training

Nexdo does not use the contents of your private calendars, emails, contacts, tasks, or voice conversations to train general-purpose AI models without your explicit permission.

We may use aggregated or de-identified information to improve reliability, understand feature usage, and improve the Services where permitted by law.

3.4Third-Party AI Providers

Nexdo may use third-party artificial intelligence, speech, infrastructure, or technology providers to process requests. We require providers to process information according to our instructions and applicable contractual and legal requirements. We seek to provide only information reasonably necessary to perform the requested function.

3.5AI May Make Mistakes

AI-generated information may occasionally be:

  • Incorrect
  • Incomplete
  • Outdated
  • Misinterpreted
  • Inappropriate for a particular situation

Users should review important information before relying upon it. Nexdo should not be relied upon for emergency, medical, legal, financial, or other high-stakes professional decisions.

04User Approval for Actions

A core feature of Nexdo is helping users move from task to action.

Nexdo may recommend or prepare an action based on your instructions. For certain actions involving external communication or meaningful changes, Nexdo may require confirmation before execution.

Call
“Call Damien.”
Email
“Send Damien the project update.”
Message
“Text John that I’ll be 15 minutes late.”
Calendar
“Move tomorrow’s appointment to 3 PM.”

Nexdo may display the intended action and request approval before performing it. You remain responsible for reviewing actions before approval.

Nexdo may integrate with services such as:

  • Apple Calendar
  • Google Calendar
  • Microsoft Outlook
  • Gmail
  • Apple Contacts
  • Google Contacts
  • Communication services
  • Mapping services
  • AI providers
  • Authentication providers

When you connect another service, its privacy policy and terms may also apply. You can disconnect integrations through Nexdo or, where applicable, through the third-party provider.

Nexdo does not sell your personal information for money.

Service Providers

We may use companies that provide:

  • Cloud infrastructure
  • Database hosting
  • Authentication
  • Artificial intelligence
  • Speech recognition
  • Notifications
  • Analytics
  • Customer support

  • Communications

  • Error monitoring

Integrations You Authorize

  • Investigate fraud

Nexdo does not sell your private tasks, emails, calendar data, contact information, or AI conversations to advertisers.

Nexdo does not operate an advertising business based on private productivity data.

08Data Security

We use administrative, organizational, and technical safeguards intended to protect information. These may include:

  • Encryption in transit
  • Encryption at rest where appropriate
  • Access controls
  • Authentication controls
  • Logging
  • Network security
  • Restricted employee access
  • Security monitoring
  • Secure development practices
  • Vendor security reviews

No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

09Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide the Services
