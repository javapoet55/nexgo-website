A few good questions

Before you come aboard.

Is this demo using my real tasks? +

No. Everything on this page is a guided example with sample content. Nothing is created, read or stored, and you don't need an account to look around.

What makes Nexdo AI-powered? +

Nexdo reads your tasks and calendar together and reasons about them — deadlines, dependencies, how long things really take, and the gaps you actually have — then proposes a plan in plain language you can question.

Will AI rearrange my day without asking? +

Never. Proposed changes are shown as a review step. You accept, edit or decline, and nothing moves until you do.

Can I use it on my phone? +

Yes. The web experience is built for mobile, and continuous voice and contact actions are featured in the native iOS app.

What about calendars and reminders? +

Nexdo plans around the events you already have rather than replacing them, so your calendar stays the source of truth for anything scheduled.
