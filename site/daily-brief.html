A LITTLE CLARITY

Your Daily Brief questions.

Where do I find it?

Start in Today for your overview and action queue. Open Daily Briefing or ask NexDo to summarize your day, prioritize tasks, show risks, or find time.

What information shapes the briefing?

Your open tasks, due dates, scheduled work, calendar appointments, and preferences help shape the brief. Recommendations reflect the information available in your account.

Will NexDo call or message someone automatically?

No. Choosing a communication action opens the relevant contact and confirmation or compose flow. You remain in control of calls and sending.

What if my day is clear?

The app can show an empty schedule or no items to report. Your actual brief depends on your current tasks and calendar; the screens on this page are illustrative.
