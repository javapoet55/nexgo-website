Real-time Voice

Say it. It's handled.

This isn't "record a voice memo and sort it later." NexDo listens in real time, asks a follow-up if it needs one, and confirms what it did — in a normal back-and-forth conversation. At home or between meetings, you can use your voice instead of typing.

  • Natural phrasing. "Remind me to call the plumber tomorrow at 9" becomes a timed reminder tagged as a call.
  • Two-way, not one-way. It checks what it's unsure about — "this Friday or next?" — before it saves anything.
  • Hands-free conversation. Speak naturally and listen to replies while you’re on the go.

Getting started

How do I start using NexDo?

Go to nexdoapp.com and sign in on the web. Add a few tasks and any events you have this week; your first Daily Brief is ready the next morning (or right away, if you ask for it).

Is there a phone app?

NexDo has an iPhone app for managing tasks, calendars, shopping lists, and important moments, with voice assistance. It is currently under App Store review. We’ll add the App Store download link here when it is available. You can also use NexDo on the web.

Does it connect to my existing calendar?

Yes. NexDo connects to Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar so you can organize your schedule alongside your tasks. Connect your calendar account in NexDo and review the permissions requested.

Daily Brief

When does the brief arrive?

Each morning at a time you choose. You can also ask "what's my day look like?" at any time to get a fresh one.

How does NexDo decide what comes first?

Overdue tasks, hard deadlines, and today's meetings lead. Then tasks with a set time, then everything else. You can move anything up or down.

Voice

What can I say?

Anything you'd say to a person: "remind me to pay rent Friday," "move my 3 pm to Thursday," "what's left today?" If NexDo isn't sure, it asks a short follow-up before saving.

Can I use NexDo hands-free?

You can speak to NexDo and listen to its replies instead of typing. Review any on-screen details when it is safe to do so.

Is NexDo always listening?

No. Voice is active only while you've started a conversation. It stops when you end it.

Action & follow-up

How does "call from a task" work?

If a task involves a person or a number, a Call, Text, or Email button appears on it. Tapping it prepares the call or opens a message draft on your device with the details filled in. You confirm the call or tap Send yourself.

Can I edit drafted messages?

Always. Drafts are a starting point; nothing is sent until you tap send.

Lists and moments

How do I share a shopping list?

Open the list, tap Share, and invite by email or link. Everyone with access can add and check off items.

What are "smart swaps"?

If you mark an item as unavailable, NexDo suggests a similar alternative so the trip can still finish.

How far ahead do moment reminders come?

By default a week ahead for gifts and the night before for messages. You can change both per moment.

Account

How do I delete my account and data?

See our Privacy Policy for account-deletion requests and data-retention details. If you need help with your account, contact support.

Trust & privacy

Your day is your business.

NexDo sees your tasks, your calendar, and what you say to it. Here's how we treat that, in plain language. For the full detail, read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

What NexDo stores

The tasks, events, lists, and moments you create, and the settings you choose. Voice conversations are processed to carry out what you asked; you can clear voice history from Settings.

What NexDo doesn't do

  • It doesn't listen unless you've started a conversation.
  • It doesn't send messages or place calls on its own — every send and every call is your tap.
  • It doesn't sell your data or use your content to advertise to you.

Sharing

Shared lists and moments are visible only to the people you invite. Remove someone and they lose access immediately.

Your controls

  • Export your tasks and lists from Settings.
  • See the Privacy Policy for deletion requests and retention details.
  • Change reminder times, voice preferences, and what the Daily Brief includes.
