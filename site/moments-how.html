HOW IT WORKS

Remember the date.
Make it feel personal.

From a birthday to a thoughtful wish, keep the people, the words, and the timing together. You choose what happens next.

  1. 01 / REMEMBER

    Add your moment.

    Create a birthday, anniversary, festival, or custom occasion. Choose its date and recipients, or review a selected contact birthday or calendar import.

  2. 02 / CONNECT

    Choose your people.

    Open Manage Moment and start in Contacts. Add or review recipients and choose how you want to reach each person.

  3. 03 / PERSONALIZE

    Find the right words.

    Write or edit your wish in Message. With your permission, AI can help draft it. Review the wording and approve it before scheduling.

  4. 04 / FOLLOW THROUGH

    Choose the right time.

    In Schedule, set the time zone, preparation reminder, and send-time reminder. Review the delivery method, then follow the wish’s status in the app.
