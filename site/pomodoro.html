GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Can I change the session length?

Yes. The app starts at 25 minutes. Use the duration controls to adjust in five-minute steps, up to 120 minutes.

What happens if I leave the app?

Your timer uses its finish time to stay on track when you leave the screen or reopen the app. Allow notifications to receive local timer alerts.

Does distraction-free mode block calls or messages?

No. It keeps the visible focus screen awake. Your iPhone Focus and notification settings remain under your control.

Can I stop before the timer finishes?

Yes. Your actual focus time is recorded in session history. You can also pause and resume when you need a moment.
