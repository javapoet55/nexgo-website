HOW IT WORKS

One thing at a time.
A rhythm you can return to.

Choose a focus, make a little time, and give yourself room to rest. NexDo keeps track of the effort so you can see it add up.

  1. 01 / CHOOSE ONE THING

    Give your focus a name.

    Choose Reading, Focus time, Coding, Write a diary, Study, or Yoga & Stretching. Add an optional session name to make your next step clear.

  2. 02 / MAKE SOME SPACE

    Set your time. Settle in.

    Start with 25 minutes, or adjust in five-minute steps from 5 to 120 minutes. Choose Auto Start Break and Play Sound, then start your focus session.

  3. 03 / BREATHE & RETURN

    Pause when life happens.

    Pause and resume your countdown. After focus, take an optional five-minute break. You can stop a session or end the break when you need to.

  4. 04 / NOTICE YOUR PROGRESS

    Look back. Find your rhythm.

    Open Overview, Sessions, or Insights. Explore Today, This Week, This Month, and All Time to see recorded focus, breaks, categories, and trends.

GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Can I change the session length?

Yes. The app starts at 25 minutes. Use the duration controls to adjust in five-minute steps, up to 120 minutes.

What happens if I leave the app?

Your timer uses its finish time to stay on track when you leave the screen or reopen the app. Allow notifications to receive local timer alerts.

Does distraction-free mode block calls or messages?

No. It keeps the visible focus screen awake. Your iPhone Focus and notification settings remain under your control.

Can I stop before the timer finishes?

Yes. Your actual focus time is recorded in session history. You can also pause and resume when you need a moment.
