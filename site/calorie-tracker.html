BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A little clarity.

Where do I find Calorie Tracker?+

Open the center module button in the NexDo iPhone app, then select Calorie Tracker.

Does the preview call me or save my meals?+

No. The current iOS experience uses sample data. It does not schedule calls or save tracking history, and local changes reset when you close the preview.

Are the targets and insights personalized?+

No. The current targets, nutrient values, trends, and food ideas illustrate the design. They are not personalized recommendations.
