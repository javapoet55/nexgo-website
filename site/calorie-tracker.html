HOW IT WORKS

Your meals. Your rhythm.
A clearer picture, every day.

A quick check-in becomes a food log you can review. Start with your goals, tell NexDo what you ate, and see how your day adds up.

  1. 01 / MAKE IT YOURS

    Set your daily goals.

    Choose your calorie target and goals for protein, carbs, fats, and key nutrients. Adjust them as your needs change.

  2. 02 / TELL YOUR STORY

    Log the meals you ate.

    Add food manually, or set up an optional daily phone check-in. Tell NexDo about your meals, snacks, and portions.

  3. 03 / CHECK THE DETAILS

    Review before moving on.

    On a check-in call, NexDo reads your list back for confirmation. Open your food log to correct details and review uncertain entries.

  4. 04 / SEE YOUR PROGRESS

    Find your daily picture.

    See calories and available nutrients against your goals. Switch between Today, Week, and Month to review your logged activity.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL

Made for real life.
With room to make changes.

  • Calls are your choice.Verify your phone, choose a time and time zone, then enable daily calls when available. Or choose “Save without calls.”
  • A short check-in, on your schedule.Calls last up to five minutes, between 8:00 AM and 9:30 PM in your time zone. If you miss one, choose Notify me, Retry once, or Skip.

BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

A little clarity.

Where do I find Calorie Tracker?+

Open the center module button in the NexDo iPhone app, then select Calorie Tracker.

Does the website demo call me or save my meals?+

No. This website walkthrough uses sample data and does not place calls or save meals. The connected iPhone app supports saved food logs and optional daily check-ins. Calling requires a verified phone number and an enabled calling service.

Are the example targets nutrition recommendations?+

No. The example values on this page illustrate the design. In the app, you choose your own targets; they are not personalized medical or supplement recommendations. Nutrient totals depend on the information available for each food.
