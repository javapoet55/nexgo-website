HOW IT WORKS

A thought. A plan.
A satisfying check mark.

Capture what matters, give it a place in your day, and follow through with NexDo.

  1. ＋01 / CAPTURE

    Say it or type it

    Open Tasks and add by voice or manually. Give your task a clear title. In a voice conversation, you can add more tasks or correct the details as you go.

  2. ≡02 / ORGANIZE

    Make the next step clear

    Open Task Details to add notes, set priority and an estimated duration, choose a project, or break the task into smaller steps. Save your changes.

  3. ◷03 / PLAN

    Give it some time

    Set a date and start time when you’re ready to schedule. Find tasks with date, status, and priority filters, or ask AI what fits the time you have.

  4. ✓04 / FOLLOW THROUGH

    Move from planned to done

    Start a task, update its details as plans change, and mark it complete when finished. Mark it incomplete to reopen it. Completed tasks remain searchable.
