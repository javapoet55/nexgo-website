A LITTLE CLARITY

Your calendar questions, answered.

Can I see tasks and appointments together?+

Yes. Calendar shows scheduled tasks, task deadlines, and events. Use the filters to choose which items you want to see.

Can I add a repeating event?+

Yes. Choose a daily, weekly, monthly, or selected-weekday repeat and set the date it should end.

How do I find an appointment?+

Use Calendar search to look for a word in an event or task title. Search applies to the dates and filters you have selected.

Can I mark an appointment as complete?+

Yes. Open the event details and choose Mark Complete. You can mark it incomplete again, or add it to Tasks for a follow-up.
