HOW IT WORKS

From “we need milk”
to a list that’s done.

Capture what you need, check the details, and take your list along. A little less remembering. A smoother weekly shop.

  1. 01 / START SIMPLE

    Create your list.

    Start from scratch or copy a previous list. Give it a name, choose a shopping date, and set a weekly routine if you want one.

  2. 02 / CAPTURE & REVIEW

    Say it. Check it. Add it.

    Type an item or use voice with your permission. Review names, quantities, and sizes before adding. You can also review details suggested from a photo.

  3. 03 / SHOP YOUR WAY

    Keep the details close.

    Use categories, notes, and item photos as you shop. Check off purchases, edit your list, or review alternatives before choosing a replacement.

  4. 04 / READY FOR NEXT TIME

    Finish this week. Start fresh.

    Complete Shopping Trip to keep the finished list in history. For a weekly list, NexDo creates the next list with copied items unchecked.

How does sharing work?

Share list text through your device’s share sheet, or explicitly create a read-only link in the app. Anyone with that link can view the current saved list until you revoke it. The link does not let others edit.

A FRESH START FOR YOUR NEXT SHOP

Keep the routine. Keep the history.

Completing a weekly trip keeps the finished list and creates one next weekly list. Copied items start unchecked, ready for your next shop.

Next week’s list is created when you complete the trip—not automatically in the background.
