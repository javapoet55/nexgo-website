Questions, answered

About the AI Assistant.

What can I ask Nexdo? +

Anything about your own day — what to do next, what can wait, where the week gets tight, why something was recommended. You can also create tasks and appointments, and ask it to rebuild the plan when things change.

How does Nexdo decide what comes next? +

It weighs deadlines, priority, how long a task takes, what it depends on, what's already in progress, and the real gap you have — then explains the choice in plain language so you can push back.

Will Nexdo change my schedule automatically? +

No. Material changes are shown as a before-and-after proposal you apply or reject as a whole, and protected commitments stay where they are.

Can Nexdo work with my calendar? +

Yes. It plans around your existing events and working hours, keeps calendar entries separate from tasks, and respects the buffers you set around appointments.

Does the AI Assistant support voice? +

Yes — ask questions, capture and correct tasks in a continuous conversation, create appointments, and get spoken morning, evening and driving briefings. Continuous voice is featured in the native iOS app.

How does personalization work? +

It's opt-in. Turn it on and Nexdo learns how long things take you and where work tends to get postponed, then uses that in its estimates. Leave it off and nothing is learned.
